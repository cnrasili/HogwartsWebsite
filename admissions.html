Admissions

Everything a prospective student and their family needs to know before the first of September

Eligibility

Hogwarts School accepts students from across the British Isles who demonstrate verifiable magical ability. Every child born with magical powers is registered at birth by the Ministry of Magic's Magical Births office and placed on the Hogwarts list automatically. An acceptance letter is dispatched by owl post in the summer before the child's eleventh birthday.

Muggle-born students — those born to non-magical parents — are equally welcome at Hogwarts. Their magical ability is monitored from birth in the same manner as those from wizarding families, and their acceptance letters are accompanied by a personal visit from a member of staff if required to explain the nature of the school to their parents or guardians.

Foreign students seeking to transfer to Hogwarts from wizarding schools abroad should contact the school office directly. Transfer admissions are considered on a case-by-case basis and are subject to approval by the Headmaster.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Verifiable magical ability
  • Resident in Great Britain or Ireland
  • Age 11 by 1 September of the starting year
  • Acceptance of the Hogwarts letter
  • Completion of the school supply list

Enrolment Process

The Hogwarts enrolment process is largely automated by the school's enchanted quill and book, which record every magical birth in Britain. Students need only follow the steps below.

1

Receive Your Hogwarts Acceptance Letter

Your letter will arrive by owl post in late July of the year you turn eleven. It will be addressed precisely to your home — sometimes to your bedroom — and will include a list of required books, equipment, and clothing for your first year. If your letter does not arrive by 31 July, contact the school office directly.

2

Reply to Accept Your Place

Reply by owl within two weeks of receiving your letter to confirm your acceptance of the offered place. Include your name, date of birth, and home address. Muggle-born students may send their reply by post to: The Admissions Office, Hogwarts School, Scotland.

3

Visit Diagon Alley for School Supplies

Diagon Alley, accessible through the Leaky Cauldron in London, is the primary shopping district for wizarding supplies. You will need to visit Gringotts Wizarding Bank to exchange Muggle money if necessary, Ollivanders for your wand, and various shops for your robes, books, and equipment. The full supply list is enclosed with your letter.

4

Board the Hogwarts Express on 1 September

The Hogwarts Express departs from Platform 9¾ at King's Cross Station, London, at exactly 11:00 on 1 September. To access Platform 9¾, walk directly at the barrier between platforms 9 and 10. It is advisable to do so at a confident pace. The journey takes approximately eight hours and arrives at Hogsmeade Station in the early evening.

5

Attend the Sorting Ceremony

On arrival at Hogwarts, first-year students are escorted across the Black Lake by boat and enter the castle. They are then led into the Great Hall for the Sorting Ceremony, in which the Sorting Hat is placed upon each student's head and assigns them to one of the four houses: Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw, or Hufflepuff. The Opening Feast follows immediately after.

Required Supplies — First Year

All items below are required for the first year. Supplies for subsequent years are listed in the booklist sent each summer. All clothing should be labelled with the student's full name.

Clothing & Equipment

  • Three sets of plain work robes (black)
  • One plain pointed hat (black) for day wear
  • One pair of protective gloves (dragon hide or similar)
  • One winter cloak (black, silver fastenings)
  • One wand (obtained from Ollivanders or approved supplier)
  • One standard size 2 pewter cauldron
  • One set of glass or crystal phials
  • One telescope
  • One set of brass scales

Books — Year 1

  • The Standard Book of Spells — Miranda Goshawk
  • A History of Magic — Bathilda Bagshot
  • Magical Theory — Adalbert Waffling
  • A Beginner's Guide to Transfiguration — Emeric Switch
  • One Thousand Magical Herbs and Fungi — Phyllida Spore
  • Magical Drafts and Potions — Arsenius Jigger
  • Fantastic Beasts and Where to Find Them — Newton Scamander
  • The Dark Forces: A Guide to Self-Protection — Quentin Trimble
