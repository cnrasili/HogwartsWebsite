The Four Houses

Every student is sorted into one of four houses — a home for the duration of their time at Hogwarts

Gryffindor

"Where dwell the brave at heart, their daring, nerve, and chivalry set Gryffindors apart."

Founded by Godric Gryffindor, this house prizes courage, bravery, nerve, and chivalry. The Gryffindor common room is situated in Gryffindor Tower, accessible through a portrait of the Fat Lady. Students who display exceptional bravery and a willingness to stand up for what is right earn their place in this celebrated house.

House Traits
Courage Bravery Nerve Chivalry Daring
Notable Alumni
Harry Potter Hermione Granger Ron Weasley Albus Dumbledore Minerva McGonagall Neville Longbottom

House Details

  • Colours: Scarlet & Gold
  • Animal: Lion
  • Element: Fire
  • Common Room: Gryffindor Tower
  • Head of House: Prof. McGonagall
  • House Ghost: Nearly Headless Nick

Slytherin

"Those cunning folk use any means to achieve their ends."

Founded by Salazar Slytherin, this house prizes ambition, cunning, leadership, and resourcefulness. The Slytherin common room lies beneath the Black Lake in the dungeons of the castle, giving it a distinctive greenish glow. Slytherin has a reputation for producing skilled witches and wizards who know how to get what they want.

House Traits
Ambition Cunning Leadership Resourcefulness Determination
Notable Alumni
Merlin Severus Snape Draco Malfoy Horace Slughorn Regulus Black

House Details

  • Colours: Green & Silver
  • Animal: Serpent
  • Element: Water
  • Common Room: Slytherin Dungeons
  • Head of House: Prof. Snape
  • House Ghost: The Bloody Baron

Ravenclaw

"Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure."

Founded by Rowena Ravenclaw, this house prizes intelligence, wisdom, creativity, and originality. The Ravenclaw common room sits at the top of a tower on the west side of the castle and is notable for having a riddle-based entry mechanism rather than a password, reflecting the house's commitment to learning and intellectual challenge.

House Traits
Intelligence Wisdom Creativity Originality Wit
Notable Alumni
Luna Lovegood Cho Chang Filius Flitwick Gilderoy Lockhart Garrick Ollivander

House Details

  • Colours: Blue & Bronze
  • Animal: Eagle
  • Element: Air
  • Common Room: Ravenclaw Tower
  • Head of House: Prof. Flitwick
  • House Ghost: The Grey Lady

Hufflepuff

"Those patient Hufflepuffs are true and unafraid of toil."

Founded by Helga Hufflepuff, this house prizes hard work, patience, justice, loyalty, and fair play. The Hufflepuff common room is located near the kitchens, accessible through a barrel in a corridor. It is considered the most welcoming of all the houses and proudly accepts students who might not fit the specific virtues prized by the other three founders.

House Traits
Loyalty Hard Work Patience Fair Play Dedication
Notable Alumni
Cedric Diggory Nymphadora Tonks Pomona Sprout Newton Scamander Ernie Macmillan

House Details

  • Colours: Yellow & Black
  • Animal: Badger
  • Element: Earth
  • Common Room: Near the Kitchens
  • Head of House: Prof. Sprout
  • House Ghost: The Fat Friar
