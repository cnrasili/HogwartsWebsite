About & History

Over a thousand years of magical education in the heart of Scotland

Our History

Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry was founded around 990 A.D. by four of the greatest witches and wizards of the age: Godric Gryffindor, Salazar Slytherin, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Helga Hufflepuff. Together they chose a remote location in the Scottish Highlands, far from Muggle settlements, to establish a school where young witches and wizards could receive a safe and rigorous magical education.

For over a millennium, Hogwarts has stood as Britain's foremost institution of magical learning. Its enchanted castle, ancient corridors, and moving staircases have sheltered generations of students, guiding them through seven transformative years of study and growth. The school's motto, Draco Dormiens Nunquam Titillandus — "Never Tickle a Sleeping Dragon" — reflects both its wisdom and its wit.

Throughout the centuries, Hogwarts has weathered threats both internal and external: the Chamber of Secrets, the rise of Lord Voldemort, and the Battle of Hogwarts itself. Each time the school has endured, restored by the dedication of its staff and the courage of its students.

Key Facts

  • Founded: c. 990 A.D.
  • Location: Scottish Highlands
  • Years of Study: Seven
  • Houses: Four
  • Motto: Draco Dormiens Nunquam Titillandus

The Four Founders

Gryffindor

Godric Gryffindor

The most celebrated of the four founders, Gryffindor prized courage, bravery, and nerve above all else. He championed the admission of Muggle-born students and his personal sword — a goblin-made blade set with rubies — became a treasured Hogwarts relic.

Courage Bravery Chivalry
Slytherin

Salazar Slytherin

A Parselmouth and one of the most brilliant wizards of his generation, Slytherin sought students of pure magical heritage. He constructed the Chamber of Secrets beneath the school before departing following a schism with the other founders.

Cunning Ambition Resourcefulness
Ravenclaw

Rowena Ravenclaw

Widely regarded as the cleverest of the founders, Ravenclaw valued intelligence, creativity, and wisdom. According to legend, the ingenious layout of Hogwarts castle itself was conceived in a dream she had after following a warty hog to a lake.

Wit Wisdom Creativity
Hufflepuff

Helga Hufflepuff

The most inclusive of the four founders, Hufflepuff welcomed students of all backgrounds, valuing hard work, loyalty, and fair play. She also contributed enormously to the school's early community and many of the Hogwarts feast recipes are attributed to her.

Loyalty Patience Hard Work

The Castle

Hogwarts castle is a vast, many-turreted structure perched on a cliff above the Black Lake, accessible only by magical means or the Hogwarts Express from King's Cross Station, Platform 9¾. The castle is enchanted to appear as a dangerous ruin to Muggle eyes.

The Great Hall

The central gathering place for all students and staff. Its enchanted ceiling reflects the sky above and four long house tables line its floor. Home to the Sorting Ceremony and all communal feasts.

The Forbidden Forest

A vast, ancient forest bordering the school grounds, home to centaurs, thestrals, unicorns, and other magical creatures. Students are forbidden from entering without staff supervision.

Astronomy Tower

The tallest tower of the castle, used for Astronomy lessons. Students observe the night sky and chart the movements of stars and planets from its open platform.

Quidditch Pitch

A large oval stadium on the castle grounds where inter-house Quidditch matches are played. Seven tall hooped goal posts stand at each end and wooden stands surround the playing field.

Hogwarts School

Providing magical education since 990 A.D. Located in the Scottish Highlands.
