Courses

Seven years of magical study across core, elective, and advanced subjects

Core Subjects

All students study these subjects from Year 1 through Year 5, culminating in the Ordinary Wizarding Level (O.W.L.) examinations.

Transfiguration

Years 1 – 7

The art of changing the form or appearance of an object. One of the most complex and dangerous branches of magic. Students progress from match-to-needle transformations in Year 1 to human Transfiguration at advanced levels.

Charms

Years 1 – 7

A branch of magic concerned with adding properties to an object or creature. Charms differ from Transfiguration in that they do not alter the fundamental nature of what is being enchanted. Covers Wingardium Leviosa through advanced Cheering Charms.

Potions

Years 1 – 7

The art of creating mixtures with magical effects. Unlike many other subjects, Potions requires no wand use and relies on precise preparation and patient technique. Covers everything from simple healing draughts to the complex Polyjuice Potion.

History of Magic

Years 1 – 5

A study of the wizarding world throughout history. Topics include the International Statute of Secrecy, the Goblin Rebellions, the establishment of the Ministry of Magic, and significant magical conflicts. The only class at Hogwarts taught by a ghost.

Defence Against the Dark Arts

Years 1 – 7

Practical study of defensive spells and countercurses against Dark creatures and practitioners of the Dark Arts. Covers jinxes, hexes, curses, dark creatures such as Boggarts and Grindylows, and counter-jinx theory.

Astronomy

Years 1 – 5

The study of stars, planets, and their movements, conducted from the Astronomy Tower. Students learn to identify celestial bodies, chart their positions, and understand how the movements of heavenly bodies relate to the practice of magic.

Herbology

Years 1 – 7

The study and cultivation of magical plants and fungi, conducted in the school greenhouses. Students learn how to handle plants ranging from the simple Mandrake to the dangerous Devil's Snare and Venomous Tentacula.

Flying

Year 1 only

An introductory course in broomstick flight. Students learn to take off, hover, manoeuvre, and land safely. Those who demonstrate exceptional ability may be selected for their house Quidditch team. Taught by Madam Hooch on the school grounds.

Elective Subjects

From Year 3 onwards, students may select additional subjects to study alongside their core curriculum. At least two electives are recommended for students pursuing careers in specialist magical fields.

Care of Magical Creatures

Years 3 – 5

A hands-on course studying the habitats, habits, and handling of magical beasts. Students work directly with creatures such as Hippogriffs, Nifflers, and Flobberworms. Conducted outdoors on the castle grounds.

Divination

Years 3 – 5

The art of predicting the future through various methods including tea leaf reading, crystal ball gazing, fire omens, and astrology. Taught in the North Tower. Results are variable, and the subject is considered less rigorous than other branches of magic.

Muggle Studies

Years 3 – 5

An examination of the non-magical world from a wizarding perspective. Students study how Muggles manage without magic, including their technology, culture, and societal structures. Particularly popular with Muggle-born students' families and students interested in Muggle liaison work.

Ancient Runes

Years 3 – 5

A study of runic scripts and their magical applications. Students learn to translate ancient texts and understand how runes function as magical symbols. Essential preparation for those who wish to pursue Curse Breaking or Arithmancy at advanced levels.

Arithmancy

Years 3 – 5

The study of the magical properties of numbers and their use in predicting events. Considered one of the most demanding elective subjects, Arithmancy requires strong logical thinking and is recommended for students with aptitude in numerically precise magic.

N.E.W.T. Subjects

Nastily Exhausting Wizarding Tests are taken at the end of Year 7 by students who continue their chosen subjects beyond O.W.L. level. Entry to N.E.W.T. classes requires an O or E grade in the corresponding O.W.L. examination.

Advanced Potions

Years 6 – 7

A demanding continuation of the Potions curriculum, covering complex preparations such as Veritaserum, Amortentia, and Felix Felicis. Required for entry to careers in Healing, Auror training, and Potions mastery.

Advanced Transfiguration

Years 6 – 7

Covers the most complex Transfiguration magic including human Transfiguration and the theory and practice of becoming an Animagus. Students study Switching Spells and Vanishment at an advanced level.

Advanced Defence Against the Dark Arts

Years 6 – 7

Required for Auror and Hit Wizard training. Covers the Unforgivable Curses in detail, advanced Occlumency, Dark creature classification and containment, and counter-Dark Arts strategy at the highest level.

Advanced Charms

Years 6 – 7

Extends the Charms curriculum into complex spells including Fidelius Charm theory, Protean Charms, and advanced nonverbal casting. Essential for students pursuing careers in spell invention, magical law enforcement, or academia.

Advanced Herbology

Years 6 – 7

Focuses on rarer and more dangerous specimens of magical flora, including Venomous Tentacula propagation, the cultivation of potion-grade ingredients, and the study of plants with Healing applications. Suitable for students pursuing Potions mastery or Healing.
